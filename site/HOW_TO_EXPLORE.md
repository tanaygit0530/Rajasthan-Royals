How to explore the interactive companion site (5 minutes)

Link: <URL>   ·   Password: sent separately

1. Overview – the answer on one screen: my top-10 batters and bowlers under pressure, each with a Pressure Rating
   (runs added per 100 high-pressure balls versus what the situation predicts; 0 = average), an 80% range bar and the
   chance of being a genuine top-10 player. Hover any ⓘ for a plain-language definition.
2. Rankings – switch between Open ranking, RR can buy, Value & uncapped and Youth (≤23); filter by role, hand, bowling
   type, nationality, capped status and availability. Click any player.
3. Player profile – ratings with uncertainty, batting/bowling cards by phase and situation, matchup heat map, form
   trend, the player's three biggest pressure moments, sourced scout notes (labelled as opinion), risk flags and how
   he fits RR.
4. How it works – the method in six steps, with the evidence behind each decision (models tested on seasons they had
   not seen, fairness checks, limitations). "Technical details" panels open the full tables.
5. RR strategy – where RR is exposed (gaps by role), plan A/B/C per role, value buys, youth picks and a verdict with
   risks and a fallback for every target.
6. Report – the full methodology report (PDF) and data sources.

Every number on the site is generated from the same outputs as the written report and the submitted picks.
